A simpler butterfly for everyday use

Feedback from the team: the detailed heritage butterfly is beautiful, but its fine linework fights itself at small sizes. This page collects two rounds of simpler alternatives. Round 4 marks are drawn fresh, keeping the brand's character without reusing the old shape. Round 3 marks are simplified derivatives of the original. In any direction, the detailed original can stay in the family as a "legacy" mark for larger, special placements.

Round 5 · The monarch, lighter Real anatomy, light touch

The same from-scratch monarch anatomy, refined again: white spots now run along the wing margins the way a real monarch's do, veins fan outward in gentle curves, and the tonal panes follow the actual spaces between veins. One drawing, four light treatments.

H1 · Soft Flat

No lines at all: hot-pink forewings, blush hindwings, white spots. The cleanest and friendliest.

H2 · Fine Line

Hairline ink and delicate veins, like a botanical illustration. The most heritage of the four.

H3 · White Veins

All pink with the vein structure in white. Graceful, modern, no black anywhere.

H4 · Tonal Panes

The spaces between the veins as tone-on-tone panes, no lines. Quietest and most contemporary.

Round 3 · Derived from the original

Earlier simplification options. Each keeps the exact wing shapes and flying tilt of the 2006 butterfly.

S1 · Silhouette

One color, one shape. The strongest at tiny sizes and the most versatile (embroidery, vinyl, watermark).

S2 · Duotone

The silhouette with soft-pink wing panels. A little more life, still only two flat colors.

S3 · Linocut

Navy silhouette with bold pink panels. A crafted, print-maker feel; the most distinctive.

S4 · The Pair

Big and small butterflies, echoing the two on the original wordmark; caregiver alongside client.

How "featured + legacy" would work

Not a replacement; a division of labor. The simplified mark does the everyday work; the original appears where there's room to appreciate it.

Featured (simplified)

Menu bar, favicon, social avatars, footers, email signatures, stamps on documents, embroidered apparel.

Legacy (original)

Homepage hero, About page, print covers, the 20th-anniversary materials, anywhere large-format.
